01 Vision-Language · Grounding · LoRA Spring 2026

GRAFT — Region-Grounded Fine-tuning of SigLIP

Taught spatial grounding into a frozen SigLIP-B/16-384 with a LoRA adapter (295K params, 0.14%) and a FILIP-style region loss on phrase→bbox pairs. The dominant gain came not from the loss or capacity but from eliminating train/eval representation mismatches (MaskCLIP bypass + EOS alignment) — raising Flickr30k Pointing Game from 14.74% to 20.76 ± 0.41% (3-seed, 6× lower variance) and 26.16% test PG at 5 epochs. Florence-2 auto-labels reach 23.35% with zero human boxes.

  • +11.4pp Pointing Game
  • 295K params (0.14%)
  • 6× lower variance
  • Florence-2 auto-labels

PyTorch · SigLIP · LoRA / PEFT · FILIP · Flickr30k · W&B

03 Computer Vision · Transformers · Geometry Jan – Mar 2026

Epipolar Geometry in MatchFormer

Injected differentiable epipolar constraints into a transformer-based feature matcher via a soft fundamental-matrix mask on the confidence matrix. Fine-tuned with focal loss + LoRA adapters on ScanNet; diagnosed confidence distribution shift post-training — threshold recalibration to 0.10 recovered 436 matches/pair while preserving geometric precision.

  • −94% reprojection error
  • +18% Precision@3px
  • 546× over baseline
